# Extreme Weather and Health at the University of Chicago

This organization hosts open data, code, and software for research on the health
impacts of extreme weather and climate change at the University of Chicago.

It includes exposure datasets developed for and with research groups across campus,
along with collaborative analysis projects. Repositories are public to support
reproducibility and shared use of exposure data across UChicago and beyond.

## What's here

- **Exposure datasets** - curated environmental exposure data developed by the group,
  including for collaborating teams across the University of Chicago.
- **Analysis code and software** - pipelines, packages, and project repositories
  supporting ongoing research.

Each repository includes its own README with usage, data sources, and citation details.
